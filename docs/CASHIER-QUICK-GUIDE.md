# TINDA POS — Cashier Quick Reference & Counter Cheat Sheet
> **Printable Desk Reference for Cashiers & Store Staff**  
> Keep this page printed and taped beside the POS terminal or cashier counter.

---

## 🌅 1. Morning Routine — Opening the Shift
1. Log in to **TINDA POS** with your username and password.
2. In the Shift prompt, count your starting drawer money (coins and small bills for customer change).
3. Type your **Starting Cash Float** (e.g., `₱500.00` or `₱1,000.00`) and click **Start Shift**.
4. Check the top bar: verify that the `🟢 Scanner Ready` badge is visible.

---

## 🛒 2. How to Ring Up Sales (POS Screen)

### A. Adding Items to the Cart
* **Barcode Scanner (Fastest):** Aim your handheld USB scanner or smartphone camera at any product barcode. The item enters the cart instantly. *You do not need to click the search box first!*
* **Keyboard Search (`F1` or `/`):** Type the product name (e.g., `Lucky Me`, `Safeguard`, `Mega`).
* **Category Browsing:** Click category tiles (Noodles, Canned, Drinks) to browse items.

### B. Changing Item Quantity (`F2` or `+` / `-`)
* Click **`+`** to increase or **`-`** to decrease.
* Or click the quantity number (or press **`F2`**) to type an exact amount (e.g., `10`).

### C. Applying Custom Discounts (`F4`)
* Click **Add Discount** or press **`F4`**. Type the amount in pesos (e.g., `5.00`) to deduct from the total.

### D. Holding a Sale (`F8`)
* If a customer steps away to grab another item or forgot their wallet, click **Hold Sale** (`F8`).
* The cart clears so you can immediately serve the next customer in line.
* When the original customer returns, tap **Held Sales** to restore their cart in 1 click!

---

## 💳 3. Processing Payments

| Payment Method | Key | How to Process |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cash** | **`F9`** | Type the exact amount given by the customer. The screen displays the exact change to give back. Click **Complete Sale**. |
| **GCash** | **`F10`** | Ask the customer to scan your store's GCash QR code. Enter the 4-digit or full GCash reference number into the POS. |
| **Utang (Credit)** | — | Click **Customer** in the cart. Pick or register the customer. Click **Checkout** ➔ choose **Utang / Credit**. |

---

## 📟 4. Barcode Scanner Setup Guide

### Option 1: Handheld USB Barcode Scanner (Recommended)
* Plug the USB cable into any free USB port on your PC or laptop.
* Zero drivers or software needed! It works immediately.

---

## 🖨️ 5. Thermal Receipt Printing Tips
* Make sure your 58mm or 80mm thermal printer is turned on and paper is loaded correctly (thermal side facing up).
* If receipts need reprinting, go to **History ➔ Transactions**, click the sale, and click **Print Receipt**.

---

## 🌙 6. Evening Routine — Closing the Shift (Z-Read)
1. In the sidebar or top bar, click **Close Shift / Z-Read**.
2. Count the cash inside your drawer: input the exact coin and paper bill breakdown in the **Cash Count** window.
3. The system compares your counted cash against registered sales.
4. Click **Finalize Z-Read**. The shift report prints automatically with total sales, cash in drawer, e-wallet collections, and receivables.

---

## 💾 7. Data Safety Golden Rule
* Go to **Backup** in the sidebar once a week (or every evening).
* Click **Back Up Now**.
* Copy the backup file to a **USB Flash Drive**. Your store sales and debts are 100% safe!

## ⌨️ 8. Keyboard Shortcuts Cheat Sheet
* **`F1`** or **`/`** — Focus Product Search / F1 Spotlight Item Picker
* **`F2`** — Quick Quantity Edit
* **`F4`** — Apply Discount
* **`F8`** — Hold / Suspend Sale
* **`F9`** — Cash Checkout
* **`F10`** — GCash Checkout
* **`Esc`** — Close Modal / Cancel
* **`Ctrl + S`** — Save Purchase Invoice / Save Edits

---

## 📦 9. Supplier Deliveries & Purchase Orders (v4.1.4 Spec)
* **Search Past Invoices:** Go to **Inventory ➔ Purchase Invoices ➔ Invoices History**. Type the P.O. Number, Supplier Invoice #, Receipt #, or Supplier Name in the search bar.
* **View Invoice Details:** Click any row to see the complete invoice layout with item quantities, packaging units, and discounts.
* **Edit Delivery Items (Authorized):** Click **`[ ✏️ Edit ]`**. Enter the Manager or Admin PIN/Password. Adjust quantities or add missing items, then click **`[ 💾 Save Edit (Ctrl+S) ]`**. The system adjusts inventory stock automatically.
* **Print Delivery Voucher or Receipt:** Click **`[ 🖨️ Print ]`** to print an **A4/Letter Document** (with signature lines for receiving) or a **Thermal POS Receipt** (58mm/80mm).
* **Previous / Next Navigation:** Use **`[ ◀ Prev. ]`** and **`[ Next ▶ ]`** to flip through invoices.
