# TINDA POS v1.0.76 — Unrestricted E-Wallet & Bills Center Edition

TINDA POS v1.0.76 completely unlocks the **E-Wallet & Bills Center** for all merchants and cashiers, removing the VIP crystal paywall badge and opening all 5 core financial tabs unconditionally.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Advancements

#### 1. Unrestricted Access to E-Wallet & Bills Center
- **Removed VIP Crystal Badge**: Stripped the `💎 VIP` indicator from the sidebar menu item and the module header banner.
- **Unconditionally Unlocked All 5 Operational Tabs**:
  - `Cash In / Out`: Register cash deposits and payouts across GCash, Maya, and MariBank.
  - `Bills & E-Load`: Process utility bills and mobile top-ups with automatic service fee calculation and change breakdown.
  - `Transactions`: Complete audit ledger with live search, date filters, and thermal stub re-printing.
  - `Audit Sheet`: Physical cash drawer count reconciliation with float target variance tracking.
  - `Audit History`: Historical reconciliation ledger with Apple Cupertino deletion safeguards and bulk reset tools.
- **No License Paywalls**: Fully accessible without requiring ₱500 VIP Pro activation or external license keys.

#### 2. Clean Apple Cupertino Interface & Zero Disruption
- **Streamlined Workflow**: Completely removed intrusive upgrade banners and paywall lock screens.
- **Dual Thermal & System OS Printing**: Direct 58mm/80mm thermal receipt printing and Windows manual print dialogs remain 100% operational for all cashiers.

#### 3. Dual-Track Hot-Patch Engine Integration
- Instantly updateable via the lightweight ~0.8 MB feature hot-patch (`TindaPOS-Feature-Patch-1.0.76.zip`) or full standalone installers (`Setup` and `Portable`).
