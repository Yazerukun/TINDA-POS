💡 ANO TONG TINDA POS?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Desktop POS app na ginawa specifically para sa mga sari-sari store at small businesses sa Pinas. Offline-first — tuloy ang benta kahit walang internet. May optional automatic backup sa OneDrive, Google Drive for Desktop, o Dropbox folder kapag gusto ng owner.

Gawa sa Electron, React, TypeScript, at SQLite. Matibay, mabilis, at reliable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💰 POS / Benta
• Mabilis na checkout — product grid at search (pangalan, SKU, o barcode)
• Cash — may automatic sukli/change computation
• GCash & Maya — may reference number tracking
• Utang (credit) — may customer picker at credit limit
• Split payment — pwede CASH at GCASH sa iisang transaction
• Hold & Resume — itabi muna ang cart habang nagse-serve ng ibang customer

📦 Inventory
• Products with TINGI units (sachet, can, stick, piraso, etc.)
• Add at delete product categories mula mismo sa Inventory
• Safe category deletion — blocked habang may product pang gumagamit nito
• Suppliers at SKU/barcode support
• Low-stock at out-of-stock alerts (color-coded badges)
• Auto-update ng stock sa bawat sale, refund, at void

👥 Customers & Utang
• Customer directory with credit limits
• Full utang ledger — track bawat sale-on-credit at payment
• Payment at manual deduction agad nag-a-update sa customer balance
• Over-limit protection — blocked ang bagong utang pag lampas na sa limit

📊 Dashboard & Reports
• Sales, profit, utang, expenses — nasa dashboard agad
• Daily, weekly, monthly reports
• Export to CSV (Excel compatible)

🔄 Transactions
• View full receipt kahit kailan
• Refund items — auto-restore ng stock
• Void mistaken sales — stock balik, utang bura
• Receipt reprint anytime

⏱️ Shifts
• Auto-open sa unang benta ng araw
• Close shift with expected vs actual cash reconciliation
• Track expenses per shift

💸 Expenses
• Record rent, kuryente, tubig, load, etc.
• Categorized para malinis ang reporting
• Kasama sa profit computation

📋 Suppliers & Purchases
• Supplier directory with contact info
• Track kung saan ka bumibili

🗄️ Backup & Restore
• One-click backup
• Restore from any previous backup
• Optional automatic copy sa OneDrive, Google Drive for Desktop, o Dropbox synced folder
• Daily backup on app start at backup on exit
• Kapag offline, Windows sync client ang kusang mag-upload pag may internet ulit
• Live ONLINE READY / OFFLINE READY indicator na may connection notification
• Backup files puwedeng makita/download sa cloud app ng cellphone o tablet
• Data stored separately sa app — kahit i-uninstall, hindi mawawala

🔐 Multi-User
• Admin at Cashier roles
• Fast PIN login para sa daily use
• Only admins pwede mag-void

🚀 HOW TO INSTALL (Windows)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Download ang TindaPOS-Setup-1.0.1.exe
2. Double-click to install
3. Pag lumabas ang SmartScreen, click "More info" → "Run anyway" (hindi pa signed)
4. Follow the wizard — choose install location, desktop shortcut
5. Click Install, tapos Run

Kung ayaw niyo mag-install, gamitin ang Portable version — i-double-click lang, walang install needed.

🧙 FIRST RUN SETUP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Pag-bukas ng app, may 3-step wizard:

Step 1 — Store Details
• Store Name (ito ang lalabas sa receipt)
• Owner Name, Address, Phone

Step 2 — Admin Account
• Username (default: admin)
• Password (at least 4 chars)
• Quick PIN (4 digits para mabilis mag-login)
• ⚠️ Tandaan niyo ang username at password!

Step 3 — Receipt & Data
• Receipt Header at Footer (e.g. store address, "Salamat po!")
• ☑️ Load sample data — lagyan ng ~18 common items para ma-try agad

Click Finish → logged in na kayo agad!

❓ FAQ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Offline ba talaga?
A: Oo. Gumagana lahat ng POS features kahit walang internet. Optional lang ang synced online backup at owner ang pipili kung ie-enable ito.

Q: Saan naka-save ang data?
A: Windows: %APPDATA%\TINDA POS — Hindi nabubura kahit i-uninstall ang app.

Q: Safe ba ang backup?
A: Oo — may local backup at optional automatic copy sa OneDrive, Google Drive for Desktop, o Dropbox folder. Makikita rin ang uploaded file sa cloud app ng cellphone/tablet. Sa Windows TINDA POS pa rin ginagawa ang restore.

Q: Pwede ba mag-barcode scanner?
A: Oo — kahit anong USB barcode scanner, automatic na gumagana. Walang special drivers needed.

Q: Libre talaga?
A: Oo, 100% free. Walang trial, walang subscription, walang hidden fees.
