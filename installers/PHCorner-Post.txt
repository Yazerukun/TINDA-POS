TINDA POS — Free & Offline POS for Sari-Sari Stores

Download: https://github.com/your-repo/TindaPOS/releases/latest

WHAT IS IT?
TINDA POS is a free, offline point-of-sale app made specifically for
Philippine sari-sari stores. No internet needed — all your data stays
on your own computer. Works on Windows and Linux.

MAIN FEATURES
- Fast POS checkout — product grid + search (name/SKU/barcode)
- Payments: Cash (with automatic sukli/change), GCash, Maya, and Utang (credit)
- Inventory with TINGI units (sachet, can, stick, piraso)
- Low-stock & out-of-stock alerts
- Customers & Utang ledger (credit limits, payment history)
- Expenses tracking (rent, electric, water, load)
- Suppliers & purchases
- Transactions: view, refund, void (restores stock)
- Dashboard (sales, profit, utang, expenses) + Reports (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Export reports to CSV (Excel)
- Backup & restore
- Multi-user with roles & fast login PIN
- Receipts with your store name & footer

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
- Windows 10/11 64-bit, or any modern Linux 64-bit
- 4 GB RAM, ~200 MB free space
- 1366x768 screen or higher

DOWNLOAD
- Windows: TindaPOS-Setup-1.0.0.exe (installer)
- Linux: TindaPOS-1.0.0.AppImage
- Full guide: TindaPOS-User-Guide.pdf (read this, lalo na first time)

HOW TO INSTALL
Windows: run the .exe, follow the wizard. If SmartScreen pops up, click
"More info" then "Run anyway" (not signed yet).
Linux: right-click AppImage > Properties > Permissions > check "Allow
executing as program", then double-click.

FIRST RUN
A 3-step wizard sets up your store: Store details > Admin account
(username/password/PIN) > Receipt & sample data. You can tick "Load
sample data" to try the POS immediately with ~18 common items.

FAQs
- Offline ba talaga? Oo. Walang internet na kailangan, walang cloud, walang
  datos na umaalis sa store ninyo.
- Saan naka-save ang data? Windows: %APPDATA%\TINDA POS. Linux:
  ~/.config/tinda-pos. Hindi nabubura kapag in-uninstall.
- Safe ba ang backup? Oo — gumawa lang ng backup anytime sa Backup page, tapos
  i-copy sa USB/cloud. I-restore pag kailangan.
